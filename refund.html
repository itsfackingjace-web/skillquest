Refund Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

SkillQuest is free to play. Supporter memberships are paid through Patreon, so Patreon handles the payments and cancellations.

Cancelling

  • Cancel any time in your Patreon account. You keep your Patron perks until the end of the period you've paid for, and you aren't charged again.
  • The fire cape stays yours after you cancel.

Refunds

  • Patreon's refund rules apply to every payment. You can ask Patreon's support for a refund directly.
  • If you were charged by mistake, or a parent didn't approve a payment, write to us within 30 days as well and we'll help get it refunded.

Chargebacks

If a payment is reversed through your bank or card company, the Patron perks it paid for are removed. Please write to us first; we're happy to sort it out directly.

How to ask

Email support@skillquestmmo.com with your character name and the date of the payment.
