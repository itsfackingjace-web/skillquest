Refund Policy

Last updated November 15, 2026

SkillQuest is free to play. Nothing in the game can be bought yet; this policy covers supporter subscriptions and donations once they open.

Supporter subscription

  • You can cancel at any time. You keep your supporter perks until the end of the month you've paid for, and you aren't charged again.
  • If you were charged by mistake, or a parent didn't approve a purchase, write to us within 30 days and we'll refund it.
  • Cosmetics you earned while subscribed stay yours after you cancel.

Donations

Donations are gifts to support the game. If you donated by mistake, or a parent didn't approve it, write to us within 30 days and we'll refund it.

Chargebacks

If a payment is reversed through your bank or card company, the supporter perks it paid for are removed. Please write to us first; we're happy to sort it out directly.

How to ask

Email support@skillquestmmo.com with your character name and the date of the payment.
