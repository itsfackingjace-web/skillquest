Privacy Policy

Last updated November 19, 2026

SkillQuest is an online game at skillquestmmo.com and in the SkillQuest Windows app. This page explains what information the game keeps about you, why, who else handles it, how long it's kept, and how to delete it. We don't show ads, we don't use tracking or advertising tools, and we never sell your information.

Who else handles it

  • Google Firebase stores the game's data and runs Google sign-in.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like any web host, it sees your IP address when the game loads.
  • Google Fonts provides the game's lettering, so Google also sees your IP address when the game loads.
  • Discord receives you only if you tap the Discord button. Discord has its own privacy policy.
  • Supporter memberships are paid through Patreon, under Patreon's own terms and privacy policy. The game never sees or stores your card details. To give you your Patron perks, we only keep a note on your SkillQuest account that you're a Patron and until when. If you use Link Patreon in Options, Patreon tells us your Patreon account number and whether your membership is active; we keep that account number with your SkillQuest account so your perks follow your membership, and nothing else from Patreon (not your name, email or payment details). So you don't have to link by hand, our server also compares the email on your Patreon membership with the email of your SkillQuest sign-in; when they're the same, it links the two the same way. The email is only compared and is never stored.

How long it's kept

  • Your save, character, houses and friends: until you delete your account.
  • Save backups: the 8 most recent, each replaced as new ones are made.
  • Public chat and private messages: 90 days.
  • Bug reports and player reports: up to 1 year.
  • Error reports from the game: until the mistake is fixed, and never more than 1 year.
  • Daily play-time numbers: 1 year.

Younger players

When you first play, the game asks for your birth year. Players under 13 get a protected mode: they chat using a list of ready-made phrases instead of typing, can only trade with friends, are never shown under their real name, and can't make purchases. Parents can ask us to see or delete their child's information, or to stop collecting it, by writing to the address below.

Deleting your information

Go to Options > Delete my account in the game. This deletes your save, backups, houses, friends list, leaderboard entry and the rest of your account's data. Messages already delivered to other players stay in their inbox until they expire. You can also write to us and we'll do it for you.

Moderation

To keep players safe, especially younger players, SkillQuest is moderated. Public chat, private messages, party messages and guild chat are not private from our moderation team. Moderators and developers can read them, whether or not anyone has reported them, to look into reports, to check for bullying, scams, sharing personal information or adults contacting children, and to enforce the terms. Please don't put anything in a message that you wouldn't want a moderator to read, and never share personal information (your real name, address, phone number, school, passwords) in the game.

Moderators are adult players (18 or older) we choose and trust to help us. They act on our behalf and only within the game's moderation screen: they can read messages (including guild chat) and player reports, see a player's age group, delete messages, and mute or suspend accounts for up to 7 days. They can't see your email address, your saves or anything else on this page beyond that, and they agree to use what they see only for moderation and never to share it. The moderation screen records every time a moderator opens someone's private messages or a guild's chat, or takes an action, in a log only the developers can read, and we can remove a moderator at any time.

If something you see makes us think a child is in danger or a crime is happening, we may share the relevant messages with the people who can help, such as the police.

Keeping it safe

The game's database only lets each account change its own data. Saves can only be read by their owner and the developers; private messages, guild chat and player reports can only be read by the people they're sent to (for guild chat, the guild's members), the moderators and the developers. Developer and moderator access is limited to a short list of accounts that we add by hand.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the game's patch notes will say so, and the date at the top of this page will change.
